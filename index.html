Our Story

Named after the physician of the gods

In Hindu tradition, Dhanvantari is revered as the deity of medicine and healing — a fitting namesake for a clinic built on the idea that caring for women well means drawing on more than one tradition of medicine.

Dhanvantari Clinic is run by Dr. Jagruti Ravindra H. Patil, who holds both a B.A.M.S. (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) and an M.S. in Obstetrics & Gynaecology, alongside postgraduate diplomas from the U.K. and India. That combination shapes how the clinic works: modern diagnosis and treatment for gynaecological and obstetric care, alongside Ayurvedic programs like Garbha Sanskar and Suvarna Prashan for those who want that tradition included in their care.

Over 15+ years of practice, the clinic has cared for more than 5,000 patients across Manda, Titwala and the wider Kalyan area — from teenagers navigating their first gynaecological concerns to women through pregnancy, motherhood and menopause.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

Understanding Your Cycle

Tracking your menstrual cycle — its length, flow and any symptoms — makes it much easier for your doctor to spot patterns and irregularities early.

Nutrition in Pregnancy

A balanced diet, plenty of fluids, and any iron or folic acid supplements as advised by your doctor go a long way for you and your baby.

When to See a Gynaecologist

Irregular periods, unusual pain or discharge, or simply an annual check-up — don't wait for a "big" problem to make an appointment.

About Suvarna Prashan: in Ayurvedic tradition, this practice is given to children ideally on Pushya Nakshatra days, though it can be administered regularly. It's traditionally believed to support immunity, digestion and overall growth in children up to 15 years. Ask our team for the current schedule.

Before You Come In

  • Bring a valid ID and any previous medical records or prescriptions
  • Note down your symptoms, when they started, and any questions you have
  • Arrive about 10 minutes early for registration
  • Wear comfortable clothing for your examination
  • For pregnancy visits, bring your antenatal card if you have one

New Patient Registration

New patients can complete a short registration form at the clinic reception when they arrive. To save time on your first visit, call or WhatsApp us beforehand and our team will let you know exactly what to bring.
